✳03 / THE FREQUENTLY ASKED THINGS

Good questions.
Helpful answers.

There’s plenty to think about when choosing childcare. These answers are grounded in publicly listed details; the center can confirm current policies.

01What are Honey's Hive's hours?+

The current Oklahoma childcare listing gives Monday–Friday hours of 7:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Weekend hours are listed as closed. Confirm holidays or special closures directly with the center.

02Which ages does the center accept?+

The state listing includes infants (0–11 months), toddlers (12–23 months), preschool children (2–4 years), and school-age children (5 years and older).

03Does Honey's Hive accept child care subsidy?+

Yes. Oklahoma Human Services currently lists the Eufaula location as accepting subsidy. Ask the center and the state program about eligibility, availability and participation details.

04Is the center accepting new enrollments?+

Current openings and waiting-list information are not published in the available listing. Please call (918) 318-1921 or email the center for an up-to-date answer.

05Can I schedule a visit?+

Ask the center about its current tour or visit process, available times, and any enrollment documents to bring.

06Where can I verify the license?+

Honey's Hive – Eufaula appears in the Oklahoma Human Services Child Care Locator under provider ID K830058324. Use the official listing link on this page to review the current record.
